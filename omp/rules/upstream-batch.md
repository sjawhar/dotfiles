# Upstream reports and patches go into the one batch, not a one-off ask

Any upstream bug report, patch or public post that needs Sami's yes goes into one batch, never a one-off "can I send this" ask. The one batch is dispatch://OSSV-382 (open to every project): write your entry to the form in its spec (what it is and how serious; who receives it and what they are expected to do; the exact text or patch; its evidence; a non-author reviewer's APPROVE, under your stream's reviewer-model rule; what Sami decides; its owner), add it under Waiting in dispatch://OSSV-382/artifact/batch-md, and tell the batch owner on Envoy (01a18fe5-18c7-7176-9c34-9692e2e168ed). This is the Theorem resolution of the generic batch rule in `skill://outside-message`. Sami's standing rule: dispatch://OSSV-286/comment/fe1d6fb9.

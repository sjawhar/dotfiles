# Re-ask

I did not see your question: you worked past it mid-message, or it pointed at options from a turn I did not read. A question exists only as the last thing in a message that stops and waits.

Ask again from scratch. Give every decision you wait on its own labelled block, at the end of your message, with nothing after it. Write as if the earlier ask was never sent: no "as I mentioned above" or "pick A, B, or C". Each block has:

1. **What the thing is.** I have not read your session history or notes. Expand every identifier and every label you made up: a PR number gets its title, a file path its purpose, "approach B" its full content.
2. **Why you need a decision**: what happened, and what it blocks.
3. **The options**, with trade-offs in full sentences.
4. **Your recommendation**, and why.

I miss the message, not the knowledge. Expand only your own shorthand. Do not simplify the substance; keep it as technical as it needs to be. Do not define terms I use myself, and do not pad: a long message hid the question.

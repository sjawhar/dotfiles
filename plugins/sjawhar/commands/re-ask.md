# Re-ask

I never saw the question. Either it was buried mid-message and you worked past it, or it pointed at options from an earlier turn that I never read. A question you worked past was never asked: it exists only when it arrives as the last thing in a message that stops and waits.

Back up and re-ask from scratch — every decision you are waiting on, one labelled block per decision, at the end of your message, nothing after it. Do not reference the earlier ask ("as I mentioned above", "pick A, B, or C") — assume it was never sent. For each one:

1. **What the thing is** — I have not read your session history or your internal notes. Expand every identifier and every label you coined: a PR number gets its title, a file path gets what the file is for, a name like "approach B" gets its actual content restated.
2. **Why a decision is needed** — what happened, and what is blocked on it.
3. **The options** — with trade-offs, in full sentences.
4. **Your recommendation** — and why.

I am missing the message, not the knowledge. Expanding your own shorthand is the whole job; everything else stays as technical as it needs to be. Do not simplify the substance, do not define terms I use myself, and do not pad — a longer message is what buried the question in the first place.

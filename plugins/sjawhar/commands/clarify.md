# Clarify

I don't know what you're asking me. Either your last message used shorthand, internal labels, or invented terminology I can't decode — or you asked me something turns ago, buried it mid-transcript, kept working, and are now treating it as pending. I never saw it, or I saw it without the context to answer it. Either way I can't answer questions I don't understand, and a question you worked past was never asked: it does not exist until it arrives as the last thing in a message that stops and waits for me.

Back up and re-ask from scratch — every decision you are waiting on, one labelled block per decision, at the end of your message, nothing after it. Do not reference the earlier ask ("as I mentioned above...") — assume it was never sent. For each one:

1. **What the thing is** — assume I haven't read your session history or your internal notes. No bare issue/PR numbers, no labels like "D2/D3" or "lane B", no terms of art you haven't verified.
2. **Why a decision is needed** — what happened, what's blocked on it.
3. **The options** — with trade-offs, in full sentences.
4. **Your recommendation** — and why.

Also remember: my questions are genuine. If I ask you something, it's because I need you to find the answer — I'm not testing you or hinting at a solution I already have.

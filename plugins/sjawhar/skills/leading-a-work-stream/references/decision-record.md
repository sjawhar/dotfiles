# The record of the user's decisions

Keep one record of the decisions of the person the stream works for. Each entry gives the direction and its purpose in your own words, the date, and a link to where they gave it. Do not quote them: their words carry the occasion, and an agent reads a quote as a rule wider than the decision.

- A skill, brief, spec or message that says that person wants something links the entry. Without an entry, it names whose rule it is, so nobody takes an agent's invention for the user's instruction.
- When a later decision changes an earlier one, the record says which one stands, and every text that used the earlier one is changed the same day; otherwise two texts give agents opposite rules.
- Never restate a direction as a stronger rule (a direction to ask before regrading his finished versions does not ban regrading), and never turn a direction into a number the person did not give.
- When the purpose is not clear from what they said, ask them, and record the answer.

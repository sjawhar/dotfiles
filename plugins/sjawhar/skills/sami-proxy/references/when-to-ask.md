# When to ask

Read before you file an ask, or when the question is whether an ask can wait.

- An ask costs nothing only if Sami answers before your work stops waiting on it.
- Judge from his last answer anywhere:
  - minutes old, the ask costs minutes;
  - hours old, give no number, because it waits until he is back.
- File an ask before his day ends (see when his messages stopped on earlier days), or it waits for morning and can become the critical path.
- A decision on another person's draft prose can wait even while he is live.
- While a block you do not control stops your work (another team, a deploy window, a person elsewhere), ask him about each decision taken in his place on a question that was his call (`Sami's call: yes`): the ask costs nothing while you wait.
  - One such decision is a lead's answer for him while he was away.
- Do not use a block you are fixing as cover for a slow ask.
- Take decisions one at a time in a live exchange.
- For unattended work, use Dispatch, where several threads can stay open.
- Do not duplicate another owner's asks.
- Do not treat silence as consent.
- Do not turn status updates into an approval queue.

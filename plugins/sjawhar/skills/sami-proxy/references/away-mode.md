# Away mode

Read when Sami has said he is away ("going to bed", "not at my laptop"). Silence does not start away mode, because he answers from his phone at odd hours.

1. Send the question to this proxy and to a second strong model, such as the `oracle` agent.
2. Decide and record each part that is not his authority, taste or risk appetite.
3. Dispatch only the rest. For an act only he can perform, use another route CLAUDE.md names (an agent identity, another machine, staging), or report the exact command he would run.
4. A step that can be reverted when he is back does not wait for him. A lead answers for him an ask of a kind he has answered before. Ask for the credentials you will need before he goes. A destructive or irreversible act still waits.

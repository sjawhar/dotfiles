# Sami's feedback on recaps, as rules

Collected from his own words on standup drafts, standup videos, morning pages and weekly recaps, 2026-10-16 to
2026-11-02, in the standup room, on INFRA-25, and in his typed turns. The count after each rule is how many
separate times he gave it. The checklist with each quote, its date and its source is at
`/mnt/nvme/sami/chief/recap-2026-11-02/feedback-checklist.md`.

Apply one rule at a time to the draft. For each: PASS, or FAIL with the line that fails and the fix.

1. **Only words every reader knows** (18). No internal name, code name, issue key, PR number, agent name or
   person the team does not know. "Nobody knows what this means."
2. **Delivered value only** (8). No activity, no make-work, no fixes to our own mistakes, no narration of process.
3. **Every number in context** (9). Out of how many, against what, and whether that is good. No internal unit
   (CPU seconds, "objects") as if it means something.
4. **Nothing the reader already knows, and each shared event once** (7). Not in every section, not in every
   stream's lines.
5. **Every delivered result, each with a link that proves it; ask the owners rather than guess** (8). A stream
   missing from the page reads as a stream that did nothing.
6. **Only what is new since the last report** (6). A number already reported is not a new gain; a number that went
   down needs its reason.
7. **Short and high level** (6). "You're substituting quantity for clarity."
8. **Explain, do not recite** (6). Each claim carries its reason or its mechanism.
9. **For Theorem, not for agents; our work, not another person's** (6).
10. **Only real blockers** (5). A to-do is not a blocker. Sami is never the blocker.
11. **Measure progress directly** (4). Never "it has not moved because production has not adopted it": merging is
    what puts code into production.
12. **Draft within about an hour of the standup** (4), not at 05:00 for a 10:00 standup.
13. **Say what actually ships today** (4), arranged in advance, not redefined at each check-in.
14. **No claim stronger than the evidence, and no rule he did not set** (6).
15. **No soft targets and no far-off dates** (3). Targets are the ones he set.
16. **Pictures only when they explain something** (4). Not a narrated slideshow, not boxes of numbers.
17. **Put it where he reads it** (2). An artifact on an issue he has no reason to open is not delivered.
18. **Plain controlled English, checked by fresh readers**, technical and not technical (2).
19. **Pacific times, never UTC** (1).
20. **If nothing was delivered, say so plainly and say why** (2).

What he praised: a before and after from production ("1,100 seconds down from 4,500"); plain scores that show a
result ("0, 0, 0.33 and 0.33 on the first 4 attempts"); a page whose rows each carry a readable trend.

# When an outside message needs Sami's approval, and how that loosens

The skill's two lists decide one message. This holds the slower moves behind them.

## A routine earns its exemption by being captured

A formulaic message stops needing an approval ask only once a skill or a template guides it, so the exemption rides on the vetted artifact and not on any one send. Until a routine has that, it is novel and waits.

The template is approved once; an unchanged send of it to the same class of reader needs no new ask ([OSSV-146 ask 81a892b6](https://dispatch.tail12ec1c.ts.net/issues/OSSV-146/asks/81a892b6-0f63-4424-b48e-51d27d8f1a9c), 2026-10-29: he asked to stop being asked for approval every single time for enrolled projects). A send that departs from the template is novel again and waits.

Captured routines today:

- a pull request and its maintainer comments to an already-enrolled project; `skill://upstream-bugfix-patch` holds the form and timing of a finding or patch to such a project.
- the enrollment thank-you and the first "we will start reviewing your PR" note to a new enrollee.

Enrolled means the enrolling project's own record, and its stream's brief says where that record is. Do not decide it from a message's tone or from a maintainer having replied to us before.

## A new external project moves off "approve each" when Sami says so

A project we do not run starts at "approve each", so he sees how its maintainers react to our contributions before we scale. It moves to "no approval" for the captured routines once he has seen that reaction and the rapport holds.

He makes that call once per project. Record it where the project's stream keeps its enrollment record, so no agent promotes a party on its own judgement, and so a later reader can see who decided it.

One named project already goes out without his approval; [OSSV-142 ask 9a66d8e5](https://dispatch.tail12ec1c.ts.net/issues/OSSV-142/asks/9a66d8e5-92ad-429b-8568-1af4d7ad5a53) names it.

# Adopting the existing work

Read, from the tracker and the repo:

- the issue and its goal;
- the approved design or spec, and what changed since approval;
- answered questions and settled decisions, with the place of each decision;
- rejected approaches, and why;
- branch, diff, CI and review state, checked against the code, not the PR description;
- unfinished acceptance items, naming the next one. Check items marked done against the code.

Carry the adopted decisions and rejections, with sources, into the plan and every worker brief they
apply to: a worker without them re-proposes what Sami already rejected.

An approved design stays approved. Do not restart brainstorming, re-ask an answered question,
re-propose a rejected approach, or ask him to approve it again. Edits that record his answers or
measured corrections do not reopen it. Ask again only when your plan changes what he approved, and
say what the approval is for.

No tracker reachable: say so in one line, naming what failed. Ground on the repo (checked-in plans
and specs, `git log`, branch diff) and the conversation. Never skip this step or restart approval.
